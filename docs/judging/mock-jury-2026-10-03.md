## Technical problems found

1. **[Blocker] The deliverable does not exist, yet is claimed as demonstrated.** README.md:45-49 says the deliverable is an interactive simulation. functional.md §8 marks FR-SIM-01..07 **D**, non-functional.md §9 NFR-SIM-01..04 **D**, NFR-PERF-07 "renders 20 000 router nodes… at interactive frame rates" **D**. There is no code. Every "D" in both requirement tables is a false claim as of now. Under Details p3 ("knowingly misleading the jury… regarding the scope of the work completed") this is disqualification territory if submitted unchanged.
2. **[Blocker] Three documents disagree on cryptography.** deck.md:107 "Real ed25519 signatures"; functional.md FR-SIM-04 / FR-ID-02 "D (real keys in sim)"; pomoc.pdf p9 "Modelled, not real… no cryptography runs in the simulation". PDF p9 also claims "In progress: a device prototype" while deck.md:118 says "Nothing else is built".
3. **[Major] Issuer-generated private keys are infeasible on iOS.** concept.md:33-43, FR-ID-02, NFR-SEC-03: Authority generates the key in an HSM and the phone "imports it into the secure element". iOS Secure Enclave cannot import externally generated asymmetric keys; it only generates. The entire escrow-mitigation section (concept.md:59-81) exists to patch a problem the team created. Device-generated key + CSR is standard, removes escrow, and is one sentence.
4. **[Major] No revocation; compromise window is 120 days, not 30.** concept.md:69 "30-day keys bound the window", but FR-ID-05 accepts expired certs for 90 more days in emergency mode. FR-ID-06 "re-registration revokes" has no offline enforcement mechanism (no CRL/OCSP/short-lived offline proof). Root key "embedded in every app build" (concept.md:25) with "kept offline" and "key rotation" (NFR-SEC-04, FR-GOV-03) but no root rollover path to offline nodes.
5. **[Major] Replay protection contradicts store-and-forward.** NFR-SEC-05 rejects "timestamps outside a tolerance window"; FR-NET-08 / Stage 2 carries messages for hours on walking people. Either legitimate muled messages are dropped or the window makes replay trivial. Not addressed. Seen-id dedup store has no bound (no Bloom/aging) with "unbounded" hop limit for OFFICIAL_ALERT (FR-NET-06, FR-MODE-06).
6. **[Major] L3 rules contradict themselves.** PDF p7 adds at L3 "casualty reporting: position + short message to the authorities". That class is absent from concept.md:164-173 and FR-EM-01; "short message" violates NFR-SEC-12 (no citizen free-form info at L3); "position" violates NFR-PRV-01 (never GPS) and the stated L3 goal of hiding where people are (NFR-SEC-13). PDF p7 also drops the hop-limit row present in concept.md:113.
7. **[Major] "Elderly check-in" (PDF p6) is undocumented and privacy-hostile.** Not in any FR; CHECK_IN is "No" in peace mode (concept.md:170); it implies continuous inactivity tracking of individuals, contradicting NFR-PRV-03, and sends pseudonymous strangers to someone's door.
8. **[Major] Phone-only mesh (Stage 2) is unaddressed on iOS.** FR-EM-06 is Sim "–" and hedges "as long as the platform allows". iOS background BLE advertising is heavily restricted, no Wi-Fi Aware/Direct, Multipeer is foreground-bound. The "phones keep going for hours when the grid fails" claim (01-system-overview.drawio, slide 3) is unsubstantiated for ~half the handsets. No battery numbers behind NFR-RES-03.
9. **[Major] GDPR vs transparency log.** concept.md:74-76 publishes every issuance (pseudonym, serial, time) to a public append-only log. Pseudonymous personal data, permanent, unerasable; conflicts with NFR-PRV-08 and Art. 17. Router street-segment locations in gossip (FR-RT-04) plus phone neighbour lists reconstruct movement; mitigation NFR-PRV-03 is "S" and "–".
10. **[Major] Flooding abuse under-specified.** NFR-SEC-06 gives LIFE_CRITICAL the lowest rate, but one 30-day pseudonymous cert can still send top-priority, all-pre-empting floods across a 10–15 hop region with no offline revocation or abuse channel. "Rate limits enforced by receivers" without shared state means N receivers each allow the full quota.
11. **[Minor] Location claims contradict privacy claims.** FR-EM-04 shows "estimated metres"; 05-aed-scenario.mmd:10 "AED 40 m from Marek"; :15 guidance "direction" from hop distance. NFR-PRV-02 says hop distance only. Direction is not derivable from hop counts.
12. **[Minor] Captive portal over self-signed TLS (NFR-SEC-09)** breaks OS captive-portal detection flows; 802.11s on ISP Broadcom CPE (FR-NET-02) is rarely available; "ISP switches on the relay over TR-369" (drawio) needs the backhaul that is gone, and the title cartoon labels routers "(HAS INTERNET)", contradicting the premise.
13. **[Minor] docs/validation.drawio.svg** says "Rotate public key every 30 days" for the governance body, contradicting the embedded-root model; it is reused as slide 5 at illegible size. `.$validation.svg.bkp` is a stray lockfile.

## Business & adoption problems

1. **[Blocker] The simulation is claimed but absent.** Details PDF: "knowingly misleading the jury… regarding the scope of the work completed… may result in disqualification." Slide 9 says built; README says "the deliverable is an interactive simulation"; repo has none. Also internally contradictory: deck/deck.md says "Real ed25519 signatures", pomoc.pdf slide 9 says "no cryptography runs in the simulation". Pick one truth and ship it, or strike the claim.
2. **[Blocker] No buyer a city can be.** A gmina cannot order ISPs to flash CPE firmware, cannot issue citizen certificates, cannot integrate with mObywatel (that is COI/Ministerstwo Cyfryzacji), cannot declare L3. Under PZP a prezydent miasta can buy a dashboard and an app; here the dashboard is a by-product of national infrastructure that does not exist. The "pilot in one district with one ISP" on slide 10 has no named ISP, no named district, no cost, no who-signs.
3. **[Blocker] State-generated private keys.** "The Authority generates the key pair… can impersonate any citizen" (concept.md). Post-Pegasus Poland will not accept a state app that holds every citizen's signing key, even with HSM/transparency-log mitigations. One journalist headline kills the pilot. Switch to device-generated keys + Authority-signed certs; the EUDI wallet already does this.
4. **[Major] Timeline/eligibility.** Rules §5: work started "no earlier than 11:00 PM on October 3rd". Git history shows substantive commits 12:05–19:15 on Oct 3. Probably a typo in the rules (11 AM), but verify and disclose explicitly; Details PDF demands distinguishing pre-event work.
5. **[Major] Polish ISP reality.** Poland has ~3,000 ISPs; outside Orange/Play/Netia/Vectra a large share of CPE is customer-owned or unmanaged, no TR-369. 802.11s is not in consumer firmware. Who pays for the firmware project, support calls when the mesh band hurts the customer's Wi-Fi, and the legal basis under PKE (Prawo komunikacji elektronicznej 2024)? Not addressed.
6. **[Major] Micro-payments and tax are wrong for Poland.** FR-PE-07 relies on a "legal occasional-income threshold". There is none for rental income; najem prywatny is taxed at 8.5% ryczałt from the first złoty. A state-run paid lending marketplace also puts the state in competition with OLX/Allegro Lokalnie — politically toxic and legally messy. Drop payments from the pitch.
7. **[Major] Liability and misuse.** LIFE_CRITICAL requests flood neighbours and dispatch untrained citizens. False alarm is Art. 66 KW, but who is liable when a volunteer runs an AED to a prank, or when "elderly check-in" (slide 6) sends neighbours to break into a flat? No link to 112/CPR dispatcher (Centrum Powiadamiania Ratunkowego) — in Poland AED guidance is the dispatcher's job. RODO: neighbours seeing "I need evacuation" from a pseudonym plus hop distance is personal data processing with no DPIA mentioned beyond "complies with GDPR" (NFR-PRV-08).
8. **[Minor] Competition is thinner than claimed, but not zero.** Alert RCB (SMS, moving to mObywatel), Alarm112, Ratuj z sercem / AED registries in Kraków, city apps (Kraków.pl, Warszawa 19115). The offline-after-cells-die niche is genuinely open — say so in one sentence instead of a full prior-art slide.
9. **[Minor] Name.** "Pomóc" is the infinitive verb — like calling an app "To Help". Poles will read it as a typo of "pomoc", which is the most generic word in the help-desk vocabulary (pomoc drogowa, pierwsza pomoc). Unregistrable as a word mark, pomoc.pl is taken, diacritic breaks typing/search. Three Hungarian names on a Polish brand will raise a jury eyebrow; own it or pick a neutral name.
10. **[Minor] Unit economics absent.** No cost per router, per citizen, per city; no ISP compensation; no operating budget for the Authority. For a project whose whole thesis is "uses what's already there", one slide with numbers would be persuasive.
